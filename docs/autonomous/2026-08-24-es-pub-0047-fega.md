# ES-PUB-0047 — FEGA — Solicitud al FEGA

Reviewed: 2026-08-24

## Current official public evidence

- `https://www.sede.fega.gob.es/` loads in Chromium and publishes `Procedimientos y servicios` at `https://www.sede.fega.gob.es/procedimientos-y-servicios`.
- The current public catalogue includes **Solicitud al FEGA** at `https://www.sede.fega.gob.es/content/solicitud-al-fega` with `Acceso en línea` to `https://www3.sede.fega.gob.es/ConRegExt/regmantenimientos/inicioAsientos.action?tramite=OFVSG02`.
- The exact launch is FEGA `Registro Electrónico` and documents the sequence: pre-completed/signed PDF, identify with DNIe/certificate or Cl@ve, attach the signed PDF, verify, register, obtain receipt.
- The launch loads first-party `js/autofirma/autoscript.js`, but this is not promoted to a procedure-specific signing ABI: the page instructs the user to arrive with an already signed PDF and no public signing invocation was observed.
- A fresh Chromium session invoked only the public **Continuar** control. It POSTed same-origin to `https://www3.sede.fega.gob.es/ConRegExt/regmantenimientos/registroAsientos.action` and then POSTed to `https://pasarela.clave.gob.es/Proxy2/ServiceProvider`, where the Cl@ve identity selector rendered.
- Only network method/origin/path and form field names were inspected; no SAML/RelayState values, cookies, credentials, certificate bytes, or personal data were retained.

## Boundary and implementation consequence

The pass stops at the public Cl@ve boundary before authentication. No credential was submitted, no certificate/private key was used, and no signature, upload, registration/final filing, or payment occurred.

The implementation is `QA_ONLY`: exact `OFVSG02` start, `https://www3.sede.fega.gob.es` initiator, and only `https://pasarela.clave.gob.es` as observed cross-origin navigation boundary. `endpoints`, `operationPolicies`, `capabilities`, and `clientAuthPolicy` remain empty/null. Certificate access is conditional because Cl@ve PIN/permanente are alternatives. Inventory becomes `IMPLEMENTED_NOT_E2E`; generated catalog remains `E2E_PENDING`.
