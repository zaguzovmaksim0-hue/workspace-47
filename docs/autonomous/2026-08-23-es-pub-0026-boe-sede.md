# ES-PUB-0026 — BOE public Sede bounded evidence

Reviewed 2026-08-23. `https://www.boe.es/informacion/index.php` is the first-party BOE page titled “La Sede Electrónica”. It publishes the Sede regulatory basis and a public “Trámites Administrativos” section. Administrative actions such as Anuncios, Quejas y sugerencias, and ARDE/CSV are linked to `https://extranet.boe.es/...`, a separate origin/function boundary that is deliberately not added to this target profile.

The first-party signature-systems page states that the BOE Sede admits recognized or qualified electronic certificates. BOE responses also expose a CSP permitting `afirma://*`. Neither fact establishes an exact signer ABI, signature format/algorithm, packaging, callback, endpoint, client-TLS flow, or safe administrative submission contract for this public entry. The Workspace-47 profile is therefore QA-only navigation to `https://www.boe.es/informacion/index.php` with no sensitive capabilities and no trusted browse expansion to `extranet.boe.es`.

No authentication, certificate selection, private-key use, signing, upload, payment, or administrative submission was performed.
