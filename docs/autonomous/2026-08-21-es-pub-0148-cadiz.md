# ES-PUB-0148 — Diputación Provincial de Cádiz — Solicitud, escrito o comunicación genérica

Reviewed: 2026-08-21

## Current official public evidence

- The Diputación electronic registry states that non-normalized requests, writings and communications use its general-purpose form: `https://sede.dipucadiz.es/web/sede/registro-electronico-comun`.
- The current public procedure catalog at `https://sede.dipucadiz.es/tramites-disponibles` lists **Solicitud, escrito o comunicación genérica** as permanent procedure `761`.
- The public procedure detail states that telematic presentation requires a recognized electronic certificate or DNIe.
- The exact protected procedure entry is `https://sede.dipucadiz.es/group/sede/detalle-tramite?tramite=761`.
- In a fresh unauthenticated read-only runtime on 2026-08-21, that exact entry showed the institutional Cl@ve redirect, passed through the institutional SSO origin `https://sso.dipucadiz.es`, and exposed the current Cl@ve service-provider action at `https://pasarela.clave.gob.es/Proxy2/ServiceProvider`.

## Evidence boundary and implementation consequence

The pass stopped at the first authentication boundary. No credential was submitted, no private key was used, and no signature, upload, payment, registration or final submission was initiated. Public evidence therefore supports only a `QA_ONLY` navigation profile for the exact procedure entry and the three observed origins: `sede.dipucadiz.es`, `sso.dipucadiz.es`, and `pasarela.clave.gob.es`.

The profile deliberately exposes no signing capability, client-TLS policy, signer endpoint, signature format or signature algorithm. The inventory state is `IMPLEMENTED_NOT_E2E` and the generated catalog state remains `E2E_PENDING` until an independently authorized authenticated/pre-sign observation proves more.
